Andromeda-Staff-Tag-V25-Pre-ReleaseV41.1.7 : 17-09-2024
=======================================================
- Updated the Battery Measurement Process

 VLL Measurement:
------------------
1) Initiate the Sensor controller ADC Task and put the main Micro in 90ms sleep.
2) In sensor Controller, switch the Power Mode to Low Power Mode and wait for Measurement Delay 40ms in Low Power Mode .
3) After the wakeup, switch the  Power Mode from  Low Power Mode to Active Mode  and wait for 200us before the measurement.
4) In Battery Measurement Process, we do 5 battery Measurement with some measurement delay
5) Start the First measurement, wait for measurement Delay 200us, then do next measurement. Likewise do all the 5 Measurement
6) For the 5 Measurement it takes 1ms for process delay to  complete the measurement
7) After the wakeup, the main micro collect all the sampled adc value and calulate the average for VLL 

VHL Measurement:
---------------------
1) The main Micro start transmitting the RF Tx as High Load and Initiate the Sensor controller ADC Task 
2) In sensor Controller, switch the Power Mode to Low Power Mode and wait for Measurement Delay 6ms in Low Power Mode .
3) After the wakeup, switch the  Power Mode from  Low Power Mode to Active Mode  and wait for 200us before the measurement.
4) In Battery Measurement Process, we do 5 battery Measurement with some measurement delay
5) Start the First measurement, wait for measurement Delay 200us, then do next measurement. Likewise do all the 5 Measurement
6) For the 5 Measurement it takes 1 ms for process delay to  complete the measurement
7) After the wakeup, the main micro collect all the sampled adc value and calulate the average for VHL
